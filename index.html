      How the numbers are calculated

      On pause, the game picks a random object that is still in the level and is smaller than your katamari, and shows katamari volume ÷ object volume, rounded. Object volumes come from the game's own simulation code, and katamari volume is worked out from the size on the HUD (diameter = 2·(100·∛(3V/4π) + 0.01) cm).

      Count is what the pause screen should show when the HUD reads exactly the target size. While HUD shows gives the range of counts you can see while the HUD reads the target metres and centimetres (any mm). Per object is how much your diameter grows for each +1 in the count, so smaller objects give a more exact reading.

      Type the count you see into I see to work out your current size. Objects within 2% of your katamari's volume show as "1", and anything bigger can't be picked.

      Close the gap uses the pickup rule from the game's code. Each object adds part of its volume to the katamari, usually 80%, and North Star scales that down as you grow (×0.8 at 10m). A suggestion is marked sure when every size your HUD reading could mean still ends between 10m 00cm 0mm and 10m 00cm 9mm. It only lists objects your katamari is big enough to roll up.

      Only objects that are placed in the North Star stage are listed (320 kinds); the game never picks anything else here.
